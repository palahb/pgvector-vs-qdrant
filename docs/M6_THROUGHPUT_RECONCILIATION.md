# M6 Throughput Reconciliation

The referee correctly identified that the 500K, M=16, ef-construction=64 throughput
values in the HNSW table differ by about 20% from the scale/concurrency figure. The
numbers are traceable and were not transcription mistakes, but they do not represent
the same measured condition.

The machine-readable reconstruction is
`results/derived/m6_throughput_reconciliation.json`, generated by
`analysis/reconcile_m6.py`.

## Exact source difference

| Source | Engine | Job / node | ef grid near target | selected ef | measured recall | peak qps |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| E1 scale/Figure 3 | pgvector | 6415792 / hamsi125 | …256, 384, 512… | 384 | 0.95728 | 1288.50 |
| E2 HNSW/Table 5 | pgvector | 6416789 / hamsi111 | …256, 512 | 512 | 0.96639 | 1021.10 |
| E1 scale/Figure 3 | Qdrant | 6414115 / hamsi33 | …256, 384 | 384 | 0.95836 | 1531.06 |
| E2 HNSW/Table 5 | Qdrant | 6416789 / hamsi111 | …256, 512 | 512 | 0.96718 | 1241.46 |

E2 omits `ef=384`. Its `ef=256` point does not reach the 0.95 threshold, so the
“smallest swept ef reaching 0.95” rule jumps to `ef=512`. E2 therefore measures higher
recall and more search work for both engines. Relative to E1, E2 throughput is 20.75%
lower for pgvector and 18.92% lower for Qdrant. E1 also combines independent builds on
different nodes, whereas E2 runs both engines in one job on one node.

The interpolated single-client engine times at Recall@10=0.95 remain close across the
two experiment families, which is why the discrepancy appears in throughput but not in
the latency columns: the paper interpolated latency, while concurrency selected a
discrete ef point.

## Resolution for the revision

- The current E1 and E2 throughput cells must not be described as the same condition.
- E8 will use a common fine ef grid around the target and estimate both latency and
  throughput at equal recall.
- At least three independent graph builds will be reported with confidence intervals.
- Table/figure generation will carry config, job, node and build identifiers.

This resolves the origin of M6 but does not validate either old throughput number as a
final paper claim; E8 remains required.
